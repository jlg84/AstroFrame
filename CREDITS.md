# AstroFrame Credits

## Equipment catalogue

AstroFrame's expanded equipment library uses equipment data derived from the Astronomy.Tools Field of View calculator and distributed through the AstroGuide metadata package.

Astronomy.Tools equipment data is used with permission and requires credit.

- Astronomy.Tools Field of View calculator: https://astronomy.tools/calculators/field_of_view/
- AstroGuide metadata package: https://github.com/tophrchris/astroguide-metadata

The bundled catalogue is stored in `data/equipment_catalog_v1.json`. Individual records retain their source attribution and source URL where supplied by the metadata package.
